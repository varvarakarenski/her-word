Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

The short version: HerWord only collects what it needs to let you sign in and post reviews. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you around the web.

What we collect

  • Account info. When you sign up, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile picture from Google.
  • What you post. Reviews (the name you enter, your rating, your text, and the date) and any companies, labs, teams, or gyms you add.

That's it. HerWord has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

What's public

Reviews and listings are visible to anyone who visits the site. The name shown on a review is whatever you type in the name field, so you can use a nickname or initials. Your email address is never shown publicly.

How it's used

Your account info is used only to sign you in and to make sure only signed-in users can post. We never sell or share your information with anyone.

Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, reviews, and listings, and keeps you signed in on your device (Firebase privacy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website (GitHub privacy).
  • Google Fonts serves the site's font (Google Fonts privacy).

Like most websites, these services may log basic technical info, such as your IP address and browser type, when you visit.

Children

HerWord is meant for users aged 13 and up. If you're under 13, please don't create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed up, we'll delete the account.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account, or any review or listing you posted, at any time. Just open an issue on GitHub and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
