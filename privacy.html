Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

We built HerWord so women in STEAM could share honest experiences, and we try to collect as little about you as we can while doing it. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't follow you around the internet.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign up we save your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google also passes along your name and profile picture.
  • What you post. For reviews, that's the name you type in, your rating, what you wrote, and the date. We also save any companies, labs, teams, or gyms you add.

We don't use analytics, ads, or tracking cookies.

What other people can see

Anyone who visits the site can read reviews and listings. The name on your review is whatever you put in the name field, so feel free to use a nickname or just your initials. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

How we use it

We use your account to sign you in and to make sure only people with an account can post. We don't sell or share your information.

See for yourself

HerWord is open source. If you want to know exactly what happens to your information, all of our code is on GitHub, and you're welcome to look through it.

Other services we use

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, reviews, and listings, and keeps you signed in on your device (Firebase privacy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site (GitHub privacy).
  • Google Fonts provides the font (Google Fonts privacy).

These services may keep basic technical logs when you visit, like your IP address and browser type. Most websites work this way.

Kids

You need to be at least 13 to make an account. If we find out someone under 13 has signed up, we'll delete their account.

Deleting your stuff

If you want your account, a review, or a listing taken down, open an issue on GitHub and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
